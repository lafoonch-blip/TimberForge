Ten logo directions

Three evolutions of the chevron pine you already have, five fresh concepts drawn from the branding guide's Concept A–D directions, and two wildcards that break the guide on purpose. Every mark is shown as a lockup, a bare symbol, an app icon, reversed, one-colour, and at 16px.

How to judge these. Ignore the big renderings first — they all look fine at 150px. Go straight to the 16px column on the right: that is the size your users will actually see most often, in a browser tab and on a home screen. Then check the one-colour swatch, because that is what a plat map, a report footer, and an embroidered cap will get. A mark that survives both of those is a real logo; anything else is an illustration.
