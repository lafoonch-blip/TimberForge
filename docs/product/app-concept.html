Executive Summary

The cruise indicates a well-stocked property with strong pine sawtimber volume and harvest readiness. Access is generally favorable, with the primary limitation occurring in the southern portion of Stand C near a stream crossing.

Cruise Methodology

Variable-radius point sampling was used across three stands. Twenty-seven points were sampled using a BAF 10 prism. Final tract-level sampling error was 7.8% at 95% confidence.

Timber Valuation

Estimated current stumpage value is $186,400, or approximately $1,420 per forested acre, based on configured species/product pricing and measured cruise volume.

Key Recommendations

  • Confirm stream crossing requirements before harvest layout.
  • Prioritize Stand A for near-term harvest planning.
  • Retain pricing assumptions with the final client file.
